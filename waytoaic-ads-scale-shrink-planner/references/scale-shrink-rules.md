# Scale Shrink Rules

## Classify traffic first

| Class | Definition | Default action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profitable | CPA below gross profit | scale if capacity exists |
| Investment | CPA at or above gross profit but ranking/natural value exists | cap budget and monitor payback |
| Waste | no profit and no strategic value | shrink, negate, pause, or restructure |
| Protected core | expensive but important for ranking or defense | do not shrink until investment value is reviewed |

## Scale methods

### Split campaigns

Use when:

- a few terms outperform the rest
- original campaign budget is shared too broadly
- a term needs separate bid/budget/placement control

Rules:

- avoid shocking the original campaign
- launch the new structure with slightly stronger control
- only reduce original traffic after the new structure captures intended exposure

### Add keywords

Methods:

- same-level scale: add strong level-1 peers
- long-tail scale: extend from good level-1 terms into level-2 or level-3 terms
- reverse from search terms: convert good search terms into controlled targets

Good candidates:

- low impressions but high CTR
- low clicks but high CVR
- meaningful orders and acceptable CPA

### Exact scaling

Use exact when:

- CPA is clearly below gross profit
- ranking push is intentional
- occupation of a known valuable word is the goal

Do not make exact the default scale path when phrase or broad can buy the same traffic efficiently.

### Product targeting scale

Use as auxiliary expansion:

- ASIN exact around 1.0x reference bid
- category refine around 0.8x
- broad category or expanded ASIN around 0.7x

Product targeting may create orders but does not cleanly control keyword weighting.

## Shrink methods

- merge campaigns when budgets are too fragmented
- remove weak match types
- reduce targets with high CPA and no ranking value
- lower bids gradually
- lower budget when bid has redundancy
- remove ad types without clear role

## Bid-down rules

- General reduction should not exceed 10% at once.
- Stable fine tuning can use 2% to 5% changes.
- Bid redundancy signal: actual CPC materially below bid, or budget runs out early while CPC can be lowered.

## Core traffic protection

Do not shrink solely because ACOS is high when:

- it is a main ranking word
- natural rank is still being built
- the term is defending a high-value position
- it offsets competitor occupation

First decide if the spend is profitable, investment, or waste.
