# Seasonality Rules

## Product types

Strong seasonal:

- large annual peaks and valleys
- demand changes materially by season

Holiday:

- sharp peak around a narrow date range
- demand can drop quickly after the event

Peak-season uplift:

- baseline demand exists all year
- peak adds extra demand and competition

## A-B-C-D-E stages

| Stage | Meaning | Main goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before A | before season starts | prepare keyword map, listing, inventory, and test plan |
| A-B | season starts to fast growth | finish bid and traffic-entry tests |
| B-C | fast growth to stable peak | scale and hold important positions |
| C-D | stable peak to fast decline | reduce ad share and protect profit |
| D-E | fast decline to end | clear inventory or maximize profit depending on product type |

## New product rhythm

Before A:

- prepare keyword map
- validate trend
- prepare negatives
- set test structure

A-B:

- run baseline bid tests
- confirm target words and placements
- avoid uncontrolled scale before data exists

B-C:

- scale only proven words and placements
- push final ranking/occupation if inventory and budget support it

C-D:

- stop promoting words without natural rank or profit value
- lower ad spend share

D-E:

- strong seasonal/holiday bulky products: clearance can become the goal
- weaker seasonal or uplift products: profit maximization becomes the goal

## Old product rhythm

A-B:

- start earlier than new products
- refresh keyword and bid tests
- aim to hold front positions before B

B-C:

- increase budget carefully
- keep or lower bids if conversion improves
- reduce ad spend share as natural demand rises

C-D:

- cut non-core investment spend
- keep profitable and defensive structure

D-E:

- switch to clearance or profit mode based on inventory and next-season value

## Data checks

Use:

- SellerSprite Google Trend for external demand trend.
- SIF keyword history and root trend for Amazon demand trend.
- ASIN sales trend or prediction data when available.

If data sources disagree:

- mark trend as mixed
- avoid exact stage claims
- recommend closer monitoring

## Main warning

Peak season is not the time to start promotion from zero. The preparation and testing window should happen before fast growth.
